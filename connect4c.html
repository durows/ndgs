How is this dashboard calculated?

Live documentation generated from current values, goals, and rules.

Why indexes?

Mission Impact, Financial Strength, and Sustainability summarize groups of related measures. Those three indexes combine into Overall Organizational Health.

Component score

For a metric where higher is better:

Current value ÷ Goal × 100

For lower-is-better metrics, the formula is reversed. Scores are capped at 100 when the rule requires it.
